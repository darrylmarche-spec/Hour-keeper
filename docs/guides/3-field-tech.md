# Hour Keeper: guide for Field Techs

**The app:** https://hourkeeper.vercel.app

## What it is

A simple way to write down the hours you worked on each task of your job. That's all. It takes about a minute a day.

- It works **with no signal**: in a shaft, a basement or a new build. Your hours save on the phone and send themselves when you get signal back.
- It does **not** track you. No GPS, no clock-in, no checking when you opened it. Photos you add have their location removed.
- You only see **your own job** and the hours worked. You never see sold hours, percentages or comparisons.

## Get started (once)

1. **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari**, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
2. **Android:** open the link in **Chrome**, tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open the app from your home screen and **sign up** with your work email.
4. Wait for your office to **approve** you. You'll see a "waiting" screen until they do.
5. Once approved, open the app **with signal once**, so your job downloads to your phone. After that it works offline.

You'll only see a job if the office has added you to its crew and sent it to the field.

## Every day: log your hours

1. Open the app and tap your **job**.
2. Tap the **task** you worked on.
3. Pick the **day** (today is already chosen), or tap **Other date**.
4. Tap the hours, or type them (for example 4 or 2.5).
5. Add a **note** if it helps (what was done, what held you up).
6. Tap **Save hours**.

That's it. If you saved hours for two tasks in a day, do the same again for the second task.

If the day adds up to more than 12 hours the app asks you to double-check. Hours can't be more than 24 in a day, and can't be for a future date.

## Photos, files and comments

Under the hours, on the same task screen:

- **Camera** to take a photo, **Photos** to pick one you already took, **File** to add a document.
- Write a **comment** if the office should know something, like a missing pit ladder or the builder not being ready.

These protect you. They are the record of what you found on site.

## Fixing a mistake

Tap your entry and change it, or tap **Remove this entry**. A changed entry shows **Edited**. That's normal, nothing to worry about. Anyone can tap **Edited** to see what it was before. Nobody's hours are changed without it showing.

If your PM or supervisor corrects one of your entries, you'll see it marked **Edited** as well.

## No signal? Carry on.

Everything saves on your phone first. The badge at the top right tells you where things are:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | Everything is saved to the office |
| **3 to send** | Saved on your phone, sending now |
| **Offline · 3 waiting** | No signal. It sends when you're back in range. |
| **1 not saved** (red) | The office's system didn't accept a change. Tap the badge to see why. |

Don't uninstall the app or clear your browser data while hours are waiting to send.

## If a change says "not saved"

Nothing is lost. Your change stays on your phone until you decide. You'll see a red note on the Jobs screen and the badge.

- **"Someone else changed this while you were offline."** Someone (usually your PM) corrected the same entry. You'll see both versions. Tap **Keep what's there now** to leave their change, or **Use my version instead** to put yours in. Either way both versions are kept in the entry's history.
- **"This wasn't saved. The job may have been completed…"** The job was finished, or you were taken off it. Tell your PM. Your hours are kept on your phone so you can show them.
- **"This task was removed from the job…"** The office removed that task while you were offline. Ask your PM which task the hours belong on.

## When the job is finished

Once your PM marks the job complete, you can't log more hours on it. If something needs fixing afterwards, ask them. They can reopen it.

## Your report

On the **Reports** tab you can open your job's hours report. It shows the hours logged on each task, and only **your own** hours by person. You can save or share it as a PDF.

## Other things you might need

- **Your name:** **More**, then change **Name shown on your entries**.
- **Something confusing or broken, or an idea:** **More**, then **Send feedback**. A few words is plenty.
- **"A new version of Hour Keeper is ready":** tap **Reload**. Nothing unsent is lost.
- **Sign out:** **More**, then **Sign out**. Only do this once the badge says **Synced**.
- **New phone:** sign in again. It needs signal once to download your job.

# Hour Keeper: guide for Supervisors

**The app:** https://hourkeeper.vercel.app

## What it does, in one minute

Your crew record the hours they work on each task of a job, on their phone. The office sets up each job with the hours that were **sold** for every task. The app lets you compare those two things as the job goes, so you can see early where it's running over.

It works with no signal. Hours are saved on the phone and sent when signal comes back.

It does **not** track anyone. No GPS, no clock-in, no live view of who is where. Photos have their location data removed.

## Put it on your phone

1. **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari**, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
2. **Android:** open the link in **Chrome**, tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open it once with signal so it saves itself. After that it works offline.
4. Sign up with your work email. An admin has to approve you before you can get in.

## What you can do

| You can | Where |
|---|---|
| See every job | Jobs tab |
| Set up a job and its tasks | Jobs tab, **New job** |
| Add the crew to a job | Open the job, **Team** |
| Send the job to the field | Open the job, **Release to field** |
| Change tasks or sold hours | Open the job, **Edit job** |
| Correct anyone's hours | Open the job, tap the task, tap the entry |
| Finish or reopen a job | Open the job, **Mark job complete** or **Reopen** |
| See sold, actual and over or under | Job screen and Reports tab |
| Share a report as a PDF | Reports tab, tap a job |
| Save or manage templates | Open the job, **Save as template**; or **More**, **Templates** |

## What you can't do

- You don't see **percentages** or **Compare jobs**. Those are for Project Managers and Admins.
- You can't approve new people, change roles or read the feedback inbox. An Admin does that.

## Setting up a job

1. Go to the **Jobs** tab and tap **New job**.
2. Fill in what you know: name, project number, quotation number, customer, address, stops and travel. You can leave blanks.
3. Choose how to add the tasks:
   - **Import a file:** the hours sheet as Excel, CSV or PDF.
   - **Start from a template.**
   - **Start blank:** type the tasks in.
4. On the **Check tasks** screen, look at anything marked orange (a repeated or out-of-order line number). If the sheet total doesn't match the lines, a note says so. Reports use the lines.
5. Tap **Save job**. It is saved as a **Draft** and techs can't see it yet.

**Add the crew:** open the job, tap **Team**, and tick each person. If someone is missing, an Admin needs to approve them first.

**Send it to the field:** tap **Release to field**. Techs get it at their next sync.

**To log your own hours** on a job, add yourself under **Team** first.

## Changing a job

Open the job and tap **Edit job**. You can change tasks and sold hours at any time. A task that already has hours logged on it **can't** be removed.

## Correcting hours

Open the job, tap the task, then tap the entry. Change it and save. The entry is then marked **Edited** and the tech can see that. Anyone can tap **Edited** to see what it was before and who changed it. Nothing is changed in secret.

If you and the tech change the **same** thing at once, the app never overwrites either one. The tech is shown both and chooses, and both stay in the history.

## Reading a job

- **Sold:** the hours set up for the task or job.
- **Actual:** the hours logged so far.
- **Remaining** or **Over:** sold minus actual.

Tap a phase to open or close it. Tap a task to see who logged what, plus notes and photos.

On the **Reports** tab, tap a job for its full report (every task and phase, extras, hours by person and by day, comments). **Share PDF** or **Save PDF** sends it without leaving the app.

**Extras** are the two "Other" lines on every job: variations (extra productive work) and non-productive time such as site not ready.

## Finishing a job

Tap **Mark job complete**. Techs can no longer log hours on it. If something needs fixing later, tap **Reopen**, fix it, then complete it again.

## The sync badge (top right)

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | Everything is saved to the office |
| **3 to send** | Saved on the phone and sending now |
| **Offline · 3 waiting** | No signal. It sends when signal returns. |
| **1 not saved** (red) | A change was refused. Tap the badge to see why. |

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "A new version of Hour Keeper is ready" | Tap **Reload**. Nothing unsent is lost. |
| A tech can't see a job | Check it is **released** and they are ticked under **Team**. |
| Anything confusing or broken | **More**, then **Send feedback**. A few words is enough. |
